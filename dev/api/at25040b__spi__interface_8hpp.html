Bus requirements

What the AT25010B/020B/040B needs from the SPI bus (datasheet "Features", section 4.3 Table 4-3, section 5). A port and a board configuration check their settings against these with static_assert (handbook KB-CPP-123). The scalar limits hold over the whole supply range (1.8 V to 5.5 V); BusTimingAt() gives the faster limits of a higher supply.

Detailed Description

The SPI port of the AT25040B driver: the bus requirements, the CRTP base, its pin enums, the SpiPort concept and the instruction framing.

The driver never calls an SDK. It calls hooks on a class you derive from at25040b::SpiInterface<Self>:

Hook Contract
bool EnsureInitialized() noexcept Bring the bus up on the first call; true at once afterwards
bool Transfer(const uint8_t* tx, uint8_t* rx, std::size_t len) noexcept One full-duplex transfer, CS low for all len bytes and high after; rx is nullptr when the driver needs no reply
void DelayUs(uint32_t us) noexcept Wait at least us microseconds (write cycles, status polls)

The port moves raw bytes: it never retries, never interprets the frame and never configures the chip. What the chip needs from the bus is below as constexpr values (kSpiMode to kContinuousWord, and BusTimingAt() per supply voltage, datasheet section 4.3, Table 4-3, and section 5); a port checks its settings against them with static_assert. Every instruction starts with a CS falling edge, so CS must go high between transfers. A transfer is at most kMaxTransferBytes long. The optional hooks (GpioSet for the WP pin, Log) are described in at25040b.hpp.
